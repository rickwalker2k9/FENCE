## Who Master-Halco is
- Master Halco | Fence & Deck Supply: North America's leading manufacturer
  and wholesale distributor of perimeter security, fencing, decking,
  railing and outdoor structures products.
- In business since 1951 (75 years). Started as Master Fence Fittings in a
  small garage in El Monte, California; later added Master Pipe, Inc. and
  Master Sales, Inc. (chain link fabric). Expanded into Canada in 1995; in
  Quebec it operates as Clôtures Bel-Air.
- Over 70 sales locations across the U.S. and Canada.
- Wholesale only: Master-Halco does not sell directly to the public. It
  serves professional fencing, decking and security contractors, and
  building material retailers. Homeowners should be pointed to a local
  fence contractor.
- Recently grown its service network, including The Deck Superstore.

## How to become a customer
Professional installers who want to become a Master-Halco customer contact
their local branch to start the process. Advantages Master-Halco lists:

- Unique products such as the PostMaster steel post and SurTrac aluminum
