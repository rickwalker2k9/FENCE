# Faith voice-over: "Meet Faith"

For the landing page or the demo video, spoken by Faith in her agent voice
(Brielle). It ends on the **Chat with Faith** button.

Everything she claims here is something she does today. Stock, pricing and
order status stay with the counter until the ERP is connected, so the script
hands those off instead of promising them.

---

## Full version (about 75 seconds)

*[Warm, quick, confident. A counter pro, not an announcer.]*

Hi, I'm Faith, Master-Halco's estimating assistant for the new OKC branch.

Tell me about your job the way you'd tell the counter: footage, height,
fence type, and any gates. In seconds, I'll hand you the full material list.

Wood privacy or chain-link. Side-by-side or board-on-board. Flat ground,
racked, or stepped down a hill.

*[Beat.]*

I lay your posts out evenly, so there's never a stubby two-foot section at the
end of the run. Then I count everything: posts, rails, pickets, fabric, top
rail, tension bands, caps, ties and concrete, with the waste already built in.

Gates are where orders go wrong, so I check every one. Walk gate or double
drive? Manual or automated? Then I add the hinges, latch, drop rod and center
stop that go with it. No more trips back for the missing piece.

*[Lighter.]*

I'll remind you to call OKIE811 before you dig. I'll flag a gate that's too
wide for four-by-fours. And yes, hablo español.

When the list looks right, the counter confirms stock and pricing and gets
your order ready.

Early morning, late night, or standing on a job site: I'm here.

*[Slow down for the close.]*

Click **Chat with Faith** and ask me anything. Let's get your posts counted.

---

## Short version (about 30 seconds)

Hi, I'm Faith, Master-Halco's estimating assistant for OKC.

Tell me your footage, height, fence type and gates, and I'll give you the
full material list in seconds: evenly spaced posts, rails, pickets, fabric,
hardware and concrete, with the waste built in.

I check every gate's hardware so nothing's missing, I remind you to call
OKIE811, and I speak Spanish too.

Click **Chat with Faith** and ask me anything.

---

## Reading notes
- Say "four-by-fours", "OKIE811" as "Okie eight-one-one", and "OKC" as
  letters.
- "Hablo español" stays in Spanish, said naturally, not as a joke.
- Leave a half-second pause before the call to action so the button
  animation can land.
- Don't add prices, "in stock" or "live inventory" lines until the ERP
  connection is real; Faith's own rules forbid her from promising them.
